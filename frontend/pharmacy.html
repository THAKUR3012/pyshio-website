Expected demand near your store

By medicine

Forecast = the last 8 weeks of prescribing near you, weighted toward recent weeks, with the trend and your busiest weekdays, plus refills of long-term medicines that run out in the window. Simple average = last 28 days × days. Reorder adds a buffer for unusual weeks and subtracts the units on your shelf (enter them in Price list). It counts prescriptions from all patients nearby (no patient details), so treat it as a guide, not a guarantee.
